Project 01 · Production improvement

Safety Box Carton Line

A production line making cartons for medical waste. Output was about 2,000 cartons per 8.5-hour shift.

The problem

The line produced about 2,000 cartons per 8.5-hour shift and needed more output. I had to find which stage was holding the whole line back.

The steps

  1. Timed each production stage with a stopwatch.
  2. Found the bottleneck stage.
  3. Built a simulation from the measured times.
  4. Tested new station layouts and worker tasks in the simulation.

The solution

Re-planned the station layout and the tasks of each worker around the bottleneck, then applied the changes on the floor step by step.

The result

~2,000 → ~3,000

cartons per shift, approximately 50% more output, and less waste.

Want to try it yourself? Change the workers and stations and watch the output change.

Project 02 · Machine design & build

Cotton Carding Machine

The workshop already had the toothed rollers for carding cotton. I measured them, modeled them in SolidWorks, and designed everything around them: the side plates that hold the rollers, the chain-and-sprocket drive, the tensioning arms and the steel chassis.

I drew the plate profiles in AutoCAD, nested the parts on one sheet in the laser software, and prepared the drawings for the chassis. The carding section was then fabricated and assembled in the workshop with a team. The feed and output sections of the full model are still future stages.

Project 03 · Design concept, not built yet

Hot-Melt Edge Gluing Machine

Double-sided tape on 12-liter medical-waste cartons let the flap edge reopen after pressing. I tested a simple glue-drum prototype driven by a drill, then designed a machine concept in SolidWorks.

The concept has a heated glue tank and glue roller, a press roller, a motor drive, and temperature and speed displays. Manufacturing has not started.

02 · Factory project

CNC Core Slitting Machine

A CNC machine that slits cores to length. I contributed to the core-holding unit and to part of the linear-motion system that moves the cutting.
