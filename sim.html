How to measure times accurately

  1. Time each stage while it is actually running, not while it waits for material.
  2. Run a stopwatch over 10 cartons in a row and divide by 10.
  3. Measure in the factory's current layout, since walking time is included. If you move things in the tool, it calculates the difference automatically.
  4. Roller: time one carton passing through. Worker 7: time the reverse fold, then the pull-out and stacking, each on its own.
  5. Chamber: time the whole batch (25 cartons).
